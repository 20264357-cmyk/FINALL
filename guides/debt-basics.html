💳 Understanding Debt

Know what borrowing costs and what repayment means.

In this lesson

Learn the basics, see an example, and try a quick reflection at the end.

  • What is debt?
  • Understand the full cost
  • Borrow responsibly
  • Keep track of payments
  • Different repayment approaches
  • Key takeaways
  • Quick reflection

1. What is debt?

Debt is money borrowed that must be repaid. Borrowing may come with interest, fees, or other conditions, so the amount repaid may be more than the original amount.

2. Understand the full cost

Before agreeing to borrow, check the amount, interest rate, fees, due dates, total repayment, and what happens if a payment is late. Ask questions if anything is unclear.

3. Borrow responsibly

Borrow only for a reason you understand and only if you have a realistic repayment plan. Avoid pressure to borrow or promises that sound too good to be true.

4. Keep track of payments

Write down balances, due dates, and payments. If repayment becomes difficult, communicate early with the lender and seek guidance from a trusted adult or qualified adviser.

5. Different repayment approaches

People may focus extra payments on the highest-interest balance to reduce interest or on the smallest balance for motivation. Always understand the terms and make required payments first.

Example to remember

People may focus extra payments on the highest-interest balance to reduce interest or on the smallest balance for motivation. Always understand the terms and make required payments first.

Key takeaways

  • Read the terms before borrowing.
  • Know the total amount you will repay.
  • Track due dates and seek help early if needed.
