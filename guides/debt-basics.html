Understanding Debt

Know what borrowing costs and what repayment means.

Illustrative image · Unsplash

The basics

Debt is money borrowed that must be repaid. Interest or fees can mean you repay more than the original amount.

Simple steps

Before borrowing, understand the total amount, interest, fees, due dates, and late-payment terms.

Example or tip

Borrow carefully and only when you understand the obligation and have a realistic way to repay.

Keep it realistic

Keep a record of balances and due dates. If payments become difficult, seek help from a trusted adult or qualified adviser.

Remember: These pages provide general education, not personal financial advice. Choose steps that fit your situation.
