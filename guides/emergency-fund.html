🛟 Emergency Fund

Prepare for unexpected essential expenses.

In this lesson

Learn the basics, see an example, and try a quick reflection at the end.

  • What is an emergency fund?
  • Start with a manageable target
  • What counts as an emergency?
  • Keep it accessible
  • After using it
  • Key takeaways
  • Quick reflection

1. What is an emergency fund?

It is money set aside for unexpected essential costs, such as an urgent repair or sudden need. It is separate from savings for planned purchases.

2. Start with a manageable target

A large goal can feel difficult. Start with a small amount you can reach, then build it gradually as your situation allows.

3. What counts as an emergency?

An emergency is an unexpected and important need. Planned purchases, entertainment, and routine expenses should usually be handled by your regular budget.

4. Keep it accessible

Choose a safe place where you can reach the money if needed. Avoid mixing it with everyday spending if that makes it harder to keep track.

5. After using it

If you need to use your fund, make a plan to rebuild it over time. Do not feel that you failed—the fund is there to help.

Example to remember

If you need to use your fund, make a plan to rebuild it over time. Do not feel that you failed—the fund is there to help.

Key takeaways

  • Begin with a reachable starter goal.
  • Use the fund for unexpected essential needs.
  • Rebuild gradually after using it.

Quick reflection

What unexpected expense might an emergency fund help with?

Think about your answer or write it down. There is no single correct answer.
