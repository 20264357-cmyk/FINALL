🎯 Planning Financial Goals

Turn a broad wish into a clear and realistic plan.

In this lesson

Learn the basics, see an example, and try a quick reflection at the end.

  • Why goals help
  • Make your goal specific
  • Break it into steps
  • Keep your plan flexible
  • Example
  • Key takeaways
  • Quick reflection

1. Why goals help

A financial goal gives your saving or spending plan a purpose. It helps you decide what to prioritize and see progress.

2. Make your goal specific

Write down what you want to save for, the estimated cost, and when you hope to reach it. A clear target is easier to track than a general wish.

3. Break it into steps

Divide the total into smaller amounts. Decide how often you can set money aside, and check progress along the way.

4. Keep your plan flexible

Unexpected expenses or changes in income may affect your timeline. Adjust your goal when needed while keeping essentials first.

5. Example

For a ₱1,200 goal over 3 months, you could aim to set aside ₱400 per month if your budget allows. If that is too much, extend the timeline.

Example to remember

For a ₱1,200 goal over 3 months, you could aim to set aside ₱400 per month if your budget allows. If that is too much, extend the timeline.

Key takeaways

  • Choose one goal to start.
  • Make the target and timeline clear.
  • Check progress and adjust when needed.

Quick reflection

What is one financial goal you would like to plan for?

Think about your answer or write it down. There is no single correct answer.
