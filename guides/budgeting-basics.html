🧾 Budgeting Basics

Create a simple plan for the money you receive.

In this lesson

Learn the basics, see an example, and try a quick reflection at the end.

  • What is a budget?
  • Step 1: Know what comes in
  • Step 2: Plan your expenses
  • Step 3: Track and review
  • Example
  • Key takeaways
  • Quick reflection

1. What is a budget?

A budget is a plan for how you will use your money. It helps you understand how much you receive, what you spend, and what you may set aside. It does not need to be complicated—writing down your money is already a useful start.

2. Step 1: Know what comes in

List money you receive, such as allowance, wages, gifts, or other income. Use an amount you can reasonably expect, not money you are only hoping to get.

3. Step 2: Plan your expenses

List essentials like meals, transportation, school needs, and bills. Then plan for flexible spending and savings if you can. Give each category a realistic amount.

4. Step 3: Track and review

Record spending as it happens. At the end of the week, compare what you planned with what you actually spent. Adjust your next plan rather than giving up.

5. Example

If you receive ₱2,000, you could plan ₱1,100 for needs, ₱500 for savings, and ₱400 for flexible spending. This is only an example—your amounts should match your own needs.

Example to remember

If you receive ₱2,000, you could plan ₱1,100 for needs, ₱500 for savings, and ₱400 for flexible spending. This is only an example—your amounts should match your own needs.

Key takeaways

  • Write down purchases right away.
  • Plan for school or travel costs before optional purchases.
  • Review your plan weekly.

Quick reflection

What is one expense you often forget to include in your budget?

Think about your answer or write it down. There is no single correct answer.
