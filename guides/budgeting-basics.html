Budgeting Basics

Plan your money and keep track of spending.

Illustrative image · Unsplash

The basics

A budget is a plan for how you use the money you receive. It helps you see what comes in, what goes out, and what you can save.

Simple steps

Start by listing income or allowance, essential expenses, and flexible spending. Decide realistic limits for each.

Example or tip

Example: With ₱2,000, you might plan ₱1,000 for needs, ₱600 for savings, and ₱400 for wants. Adjust these amounts to your real situation.

Keep it realistic

Review your plan weekly. If a category is too low, make an adjustment instead of giving up.

Remember: These pages provide general education, not personal financial advice. Choose steps that fit your situation.
