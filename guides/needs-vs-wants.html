🛒 Needs vs. Wants

Pause and decide whether a purchase fits your priorities.

In this lesson

Learn the basics, see an example, and try a quick reflection at the end.

  • What is a need?
  • What is a want?
  • Some choices depend on the situation
  • Ask before buying
  • Example
  • Key takeaways
  • Quick reflection

1. What is a need?

Needs are things important for health, safety, and daily responsibilities, such as food, basic clothing, transportation, housing, and school requirements.

2. What is a want?

Wants are things that can make life more enjoyable but are not essential right now. Examples may include extra accessories, upgrades, or entertainment.

3. Some choices depend on the situation

The same item can be a need for one person and a want for another. Consider your circumstances, responsibilities, and available options.

4. Ask before buying

Will I use it? Do I already have something that works? Does it fit my budget? Would waiting help me decide? These questions can prevent rushed spending.

5. Example

A basic phone needed for school communication may be a need. A newer model when your current phone works may be a want. The important thing is considering your own needs and budget.

Example to remember

A basic phone needed for school communication may be a need. A newer model when your current phone works may be a want. The important thing is considering your own needs and budget.

Key takeaways

  • Prioritize essential needs.
  • Wait before making non-urgent purchases.
  • Compare options and consider whether you already own something similar.

Quick reflection

Think of a recent purchase. Was it a need, a want, or a mix of both?

Think about your answer or write it down. There is no single correct answer.
